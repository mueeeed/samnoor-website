Reading the GSM tables

  • GSM (grams per square metre) is fabric weight. Higher GSM means more opacity, a heavier drape and a higher price.
  • The row marked standard is the weight we stock and recommend for that style.
  • Silk is graded in momme (mm). 1 momme ≈ 4.3 GSM, so 19 mm ≈ 82 GSM.
  • Abaya prices cover sizes 52 – 58. Sizes 60 – 62 add about ₹40 – ₹80 per piece. Two- and three-piece sets are priced per complete set.
  • USD figures are converted at ₹88 = US$1 and follow the exchange rate on the quotation date.

Abaya fabric & GSM guide

Zoom120 – 140 GSM

Zoom is a lightweight, breathable alternative to Nida, better suited to warmer climates and everyday wear where a lighter hand-feel matters more than structure. It takes color well too, which is part of why it's such a common choice for value-tier abaya and khimar programs.

LightweightBreathableCost-efficientWide color range

Nida150 – 185 GSM

Nida is the workhorse of our abaya program — a matte, medium-weight polyester weave with enough body to hold its structure without turning stiff. It resists creasing through transit and daily wear, which is exactly why it's our default base fabric for volume wholesale orders.

Matte finishWrinkle-resistantOpaque at standard weightHolds structured seams

Korean Nida160 – 210 GSM

Korean Nida uses a finer, more tightly spun yarn than standard Nida, which gives it a slightly higher sheen, a smoother hand-feel, and better drape recovery. We recommend it as the upgrade fabric for private label ranges positioned above entry-level pricing.

Smoother hand-feelImproved drapeSubtle sheenExcellent color depth

Crepe200 – 240 GSM

Crepe is our most versatile occasion fabric, and we use it across khimars and embellished crepe abayas. The slightly textured surface diffuses light attractively, which is part of why it's a preferred base for embroidery and stonework.

Textured matte finishHolds embellishment wellGood structureAvailable satin-back

Linen-Cotton150 – 180 GSM

Linen and linen blends show up in our warm-climate and resort-adjacent modest wear lines. We finish our blends specifically to cut down on the extreme creasing pure linen is known for, while keeping its characteristic texture and breathability intact.

Highly breathableTextured hand-feelNaturally coolingReduced-crease finish

Satin110 – 130 GSM

High-sheen satin base for bridal and evening abayas. Fluid, photograph-ready drape that carries embellishment weight.

Mulberry Silk19 – 22 momme (≈82 – 95 GSM)

We reserve mulberry silk for our luxury tier — hand-rolled hem hijabs, premium scarves, and gift-ready ranges. It carries a natural sheen and drape that synthetic fabrics just can't fully replicate, and we inspect every roll for consistency before it gets cut.

Abaya Styles

Everyday Nida abayas through to hand-embellished occasion pieces, cut and finished at our Mumbai facility to whatever private label spec you send us.

Abaya is the biggest single line running through our factory, and it's also where cut matters just as much as fabric does. An open-front Nida abaya and an embellished crepe occasion piece share almost nothing in terms of pattern block, seam allowance, or finishing standard, even though a buyer might list both under "abayas" on the same purchase order.

Our everyday abaya program is built on matte Korean Nida, a fabric with enough body to hold its shape without turning stiff. We finish it with concealed snap closures and reinforced pockets at the side seam, and this is the style our GCC and South Asian accounts come back for order after order. Above that sits our occasion tier: heavier crepe bases that can carry hand-placed stonework and embroidery without the drape distorting, produced in smaller runs starting at 25 pieces per pattern per colour, which tends to suit boutique and bridal-adjacent buyers better than a bulk-volume minimum would.

Custom embroidery placement, woven size labels, and custom snap or zip closures are all available across the range for private label orders. Size runs go from 52 to 62 as standard, and we'll do made-to-measure for occasion pieces on request.

Closed Abaya

Fully closed silhouette with a hidden zip or pull-over construction and no front opening.

Closed abayas are cut as one continuous piece, no front seam to close, using either a concealed back zip or a pull-over head opening depending on how heavy the fabric is. Taking away the front closure also takes away the most common failure point on an abaya, a snap or zip pull that gives out after repeated use, and that's part of why this silhouette shows the lowest return rate across our range according to distributor feedback. It's the volume style most GCC and South Asian accounts keep reordering season after season, usually run on matte Korean Nida for enough body to hold structure without turning stiff. Offered from 25 pieces per pattern per colour, with reinforced side-seam pockets as standard.

Open Abaya

Open-front layering abaya worn over an underdress rather than closed at the front.

Open abayas are a layering piece from the start: cut with a front opening and no closure, meant to be worn over an underdress, trousers, or a coordinating set rather than sealed shut. The wider armhole and looser body give more visible movement than a closed abaya, which is part of why this silhouette tends to photograph better for social media and e-commerce than it does hanging on a rack. Most buyers pair it with a matching underdress SKU to sell as a two-piece look, without actually manufacturing it as a literal two-piece set. Offered from 25 pieces per pattern per colour.

Occasion-grade abayas with embroidery, pearl, stone, and crystal embellishment on silk and satin bases, made for wedding, nikah, and bridal party ranges.

Bridal abaya is the most heavily embellished, highest-margin tier in our abaya range, and it's also where fabric weight matters more than anywhere else. Every stone, pearl, or crystal adds real weight to the garment, and a base fabric that can't hold that weight will sag or pucker right where the embellishment sits. That's why we only build this range on heavier crepe, satin, and silk bases, never on the lighter fabrics we'd use for everyday abayas.

Buyers generally use this category alongside their standard abaya range, to cover boutique, bridal party, and gifting channels — lower volume, better margin. Small-batch production is standard, most styles are available from 25 pieces per pattern per colour, and if you're building a signature bridal line, custom embroidery motifs, stone placement, and color-matched threadwork are all on the table for private label.

Bridal Embroidered Abaya

Hand or machine thread embroidery in a custom motif on a heavier crepe or satin base.

Bridal embroidered abayas carry a thread-embroidered motif across the bodice, sleeves, or hem — a buyer's own pattern, or one we develop in-house from a reference image. We deliberately use a heavier crepe or satin base here, since it holds the embroidery's tension without puckering around the stitched area, which is the defect most buyers tell us they've run into with lower-cost embroidered competitors. This style tends to get ordered as a fully custom, one-off design for a specific wedding party rather than as a repeat catalog SKU. Produced from 25 pieces per pattern per colour.
