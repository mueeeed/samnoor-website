Reading the GSM tables

  • GSM (grams per square metre) is fabric weight. Higher GSM means more opacity, a heavier drape and a higher price.
  • The row marked standard is the weight we stock and recommend for that style.
  • Silk is graded in momme (mm). 1 momme ≈ 4.3 GSM, so 19 mm ≈ 82 GSM.
  • Hijab prices are quoted at 75×190 cm. Allow about −7% for 70×180 cm and +12% for 80×200 cm. Silk squares are priced at 90×90 cm.
  • USD figures are converted at ₹88 = US$1 and follow the exchange rate on the quotation date.

Core Hijabs

Chiffon, jersey, modal, and Nida hijabs, finished for a drape and hand-feel that holds up whether you're ordering 25 pieces or 50,000.

Hijab is usually the first category a new wholesale buyer orders from us, and it's also where fabric choice matters more than people expect going in. We work with four fabric families here: chiffon, jersey, modal, and Nida. Each one solves a different problem for a different kind of customer, and picking one purely because it's cheaper per meter is a decision that tends to show up in your return rate a few months later.

Chiffon is our highest-volume export fabric — a 75 gsm matte weave, opaque enough to wear without an underscarf in most climates. It's what most buyers actually mean when they ask for a "standard" hijab, even if they don't use that word. Jersey and modal cover the no-pin, wrap-and-go side of things: four-way stretch, no fraying at the edges, and a softer feel that tends to do better in cooler markets than chiffon does. Nida sits at the more structured end of the range, and it's usually ordered by buyers who are already stocking our Nida abayas and want the headscarf to match.

Every hijab ships in our standard 42-shade library. Custom Pantone matching is available on request if you need something outside that range. Standard sizes are 70x180 cm, 75x190 cm, and 80x200 cm, and we can cut custom dimensions on request for markets that want something shorter or fuller.

Georgette Hijabs

Crinkle-texture georgette with visible movement and a lightweight, non-cling drape.

This is usually what we point buyers toward when they want more visible texture than chiffon gives them, without adding any weight to the fabric. It's a self-crinkled polyester-viscose weave, and the fold is soft right out of the pack, no steaming needed before a customer can wear it, which also means it photographs well straight off a hanger. Because the crinkle is woven in rather than pressed on, it doesn't flatten out after a few washes the way pleated finishes tend to, and that's the complaint we hear most often about competitors' versions. Offered from 25 pieces per pattern per colour, and we'll match a specific Pantone if you need one.
